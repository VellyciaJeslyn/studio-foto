About Us

Pixelora Studio

Welcome to Pixelora Studio! We are here to help you capture your precious moments through a fun and enjoyable photography experience. Founded by Hirono in 2026, Pixelora Studio is located in Pontianak and offers a variety of room concepts, trendy accessories, and our signature touch of blue to create a unique and memorable photoshoot experience for everyone

Featuring a modern, pastel-toned interior with blue, white, and pink as our main colors, we provide a comfortable photo space for personal, family, graduation, and product sessions. Our studio is open from 09.00 AM to 08.00 PM WIB, welcoming everyone to enjoy their photography experience.

Our Story

Pixelora Studio was established in 2026 in Pontianak by Hirono. We believe that a simple photograph can become a meaningful memory when captured at the right moment. With different room concepts and a variety of trendy accessories, we aim to make every photoshoot special and enjoyable.
